# Numerical Computing

## Optimization

Experience includes stochastic and deterministic optimization for parameter estimation and inverse problems, including circuit parameters from noisy I–V data, microstructure reconstruction and SEM image registration.

## Modeling

Academic and industrial work has included coupled thermo-mechanical modelling, finite-element methods, phase-transformation modelling, thermal and flow fields and PDE-based numerical methods.

## Tools

C++, Python, MATLAB, Octave, Armadillo, OpenBLAS, OpenMP, NumPy/SciPy, Maxima and Maple.
