# Algorithms & Scientific Software
## Moving Wave Front
A **Moving Wave Front image-processing algorithm** was developed for microstructural quantification of Tungsten Heavy Alloy and published in *Computational Materials Science* in 2019.
## SEM image montage generation
At NCAIR, IIT Bombay, I developed a MATLAB algorithm and GUI for automatically generating montages from multiple SEM images, combining stochastic search, steepest-descent optimization and image processing. The work was presented at IMTEX-2014.
## Parameter estimation
Earlier work involved estimating five equivalent-circuit parameters for Dye-Sensitized Solar Cells directly from noisy experimental current-voltage data using a stochastic-search algorithm.
## A recurring pattern
```text
Measurements / images / statistics
                ↓
          Preprocessing
                ↓
       Mathematical model
                ↓
      Search / optimization
                ↓
       Parameter estimation
                ↓
       Physical interpretation
```
