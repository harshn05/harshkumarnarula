# Synthetic Polycrystalline Microstructures
## From statistics to structure
Polycrystalline materials can be represented by collections of grains with spatial relationships. Complete 3D structural information is often unavailable, while descriptors such as grain volumes and centroids may be available.
The research explored how sparse descriptors can be used to generate or reconstruct plausible 3D structures.
## REvoSim
**REvoSim** is a recursive algorithm developed to reconstruct 3D polycrystalline structure purely from **grain-volume and centroid statistics**. The problem can be viewed as an inverse problem under uncertainty.
## Tessellation
The research developed a unified tessellation model and quantified the significance of fitting parameters in existing tessellation approaches.
## Why synthetic microstructures matter
Synthetic structures provide controlled test cases for microstructure analysis, numerical simulation, algorithm validation, sensitivity studies and computational materials modelling.
